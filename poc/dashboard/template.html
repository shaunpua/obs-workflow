Lead Leak Monitor

The whole flow

Meta sends your server a webhook for every customer message and for every reply your Page sends. Your server keeps a copy, works out who the lead is, and updates the metrics, the hot/warm/cold label and the next action. It also checks whether an alert should go out.

flowchart LR
  C([Customer]) -->|DM / WhatsApp| M[Meta servers]
  S([Staff in inbox / phone]) -->|reply| M
  AI([Your AI responder]) -->|reply via Send API| M
  M -->|webhook: messages| W[Your webhook endpoint]
  M -->|webhook: message_echoes| W
  W --> R[(raw_webhooks)]
  R --> N[Normalize to one shape]
  N --> L[(leads)]
  N --> MS[(messages)]
  N --> EV[(events = traces)]
  MS --> MET[Metrics views: reply time p50/p90]
  MS --> CL[Classifier: rules + optional LLM]
  CL --> L
  L --> NA[Next action]
  L --> AL[Alert check every minute]
  AL -->|Telegram / Viber| S
  MET --> D[Dashboard]
  NA --> D
  EV --> D

What you get from each webhook

Metric logs

Every message has a timestamp. Reply time is your reply minus the first unanswered customer message.

Classification

Keyword signals score each lead hot, warm or cold. An LLM is only asked about unclear cases.

Traces

Each lead's messages and events in time order. The gaps between them show where time goes.

Bottlenecks

Funnel drop-off per stage and the median time spent in each step.

Actions

Ordered rules turn state into "what to do now", e.g. "Hot, waiting: offer 2 slots".

Alerts

A hot lead unanswered for 10 minutes pings the front desk. At 30 minutes it goes to the manager.

A lead's stages (clinic config)

Stages come from a YAML file per niche, so solar or real estate only needs a different file.

Test it with your own Facebook Page

  1. Run this kit on your laptop: Postgres plus the Python app.
  2. Expose it with a free tunnel such as cloudflared or ngrok, which gives you an https URL.
  3. Create a Meta app (Business type), add Messenger, and paste the URL and verify token into Webhooks.
  4. Generate a Page token and subscribe the Page to messages and message_echoes.
  5. Message your Page from your personal account, then reply from Business Suite.
  6. Run build_dashboard and both timestamps appear here.

Development mode only receives messages from people with a role on the app (you, or testers you add). That is enough for testing, and no app review is needed.
